About me

Computational cancer genomics and translational oncology

I am a computational cancer genomicist and Postdoctoral Associate in the Lee–Oesterreich Laboratory at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center, USA. I earned my Ph.D. in Breast Cancer Genomics from the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, India.

My research focuses on understanding cancer heterogeneity, tumor evolution, and therapeutic resistance through next-generation sequencing, liquid biopsy, and integrative multi-omics approaches. I analyze genomic and transcriptomic data from patient-derived tumors, longitudinal plasma samples, and experimental models to investigate molecular alterations and their clinical relevance.

My work spans whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing, targeted sequencing, circulating cell-free DNA analysis, structural variation, bulk and single-cell transcriptomics, spatial profiling, and optical genome mapping. I combine computational analyses with experimental validation and develop reproducible workflows in R, Python, and Bash to support translational cancer research.

Research expertise

Scientific and computational expertise

Cancer Genomics & Translational Oncology

Somatic mutation profiling, copy-number and structural variant analysis, gene fusions, genomic instability, tumor evolution, therapeutic resistance, and biomarker discovery in clinically annotated cancer cohorts.

Liquid Biopsy & Circulating Tumor DNA

Longitudinal cfDNA/ctDNA profiling, ultra-low-pass whole-genome sequencing, tumor fraction estimation, fragmentomics, end-motif analysis, and nucleosome-associated signals.

Transcriptomics & Multi-Omics Integration

Bulk and single-cell RNA sequencing, spatial transcriptomics, differential gene expression, pathway enrichment, immune deconvolution, molecular subtyping, and integration of genomic, transcriptomic, and clinical data.

Computational Biology & Bioinformatics

Development of reproducible NGS analysis workflows, statistical modeling, unsupervised clustering, non-negative matrix factorization (NMF), multi-omics factor analysis (MOFA), and interpretable machine learning.

Molecular Modeling & Dynamics Simulations

Structure-based molecular analysis, virtual screening, molecular dynamics simulations, and computational investigation of protein variants and their functional consequences.

Molecular & Experimental Genomics

NGS library preparation, droplet digital PCR, Sanger sequencing, molecular validation of genomic alterations, and integration of patient-derived organoid models.
